# Open&minimal privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026. [Türkçe](PRIVACY.tr.md).

Open&minimal is a local-first Android launcher. The app itself has no Internet permission, account, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, or remote service. We do not receive your launcher data.

The app stores settings, favorite apps and folders, notes, tasks, widget bindings, custom presets, screen-time rules and usage summaries on your device. Android Usage Access is optional and supplies app activity used locally for screen-time estimates and daily limits. The optional Live Focus Guard accessibility service observes foreground app changes to show a gate when a rule applies; it does not retrieve or transmit screen text or content. Android system consent is required for each permission. Turning off Live Focus Guard stops its foreground monitoring; disabling Usage Access stops new usage estimates.

Open&minimal can open apps, Android widgets, system settings and Obsidian links at your request. Those third-party apps or widgets may use the network and are governed by their own policies. Selecting a local photo as wallpaper grants access to that selected photo for display. The onboarding online preference does not enable a connection in the current version.

Android cloud backup for this app is disabled. To remove stored data, use Android Settings → Apps → Open&minimal → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes your notes, tasks, rules and presets. No in-app account exists to delete.

This policy describes the current app version. Changes to future versions will be recorded here. Privacy questions and bug reports can be filed in the project's GitHub issue tracker.
